Ferns N Petals — Sales Analysis

1 Jan – 29 Dec 2023
The finding
Four festival months carry
68% of the year's revenue.

Valentine's, Holi, Raksha Bandhan and Diwali compress a gifting business into four narrow windows. The other eight months run at roughly a fifth of that pace — which makes inventory, staffing and delivery capacity a calendar problem before it is a demand problem.
